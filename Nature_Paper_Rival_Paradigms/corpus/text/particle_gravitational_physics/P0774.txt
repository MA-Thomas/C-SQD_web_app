The two-photon widths I » of the rl„Zo,and X2 are of
interest for understanding the dynamics of charmonium
states. In a nonrelativistic potential model, one consid-
ers the rl, as a cc 'So bound state with the same radial
wave function as the J/tlr; the electromagnetic width of
the J/tlr then implies I »(rl, ) = 6.7 keV. Such a model
also gives relations between electromagnetic and hadron-
ic widths, leading to predictions of I » for the PJ states
Xo and X2, though with larger uncertainties. Calculations
of I » have also been made in relativistic potential mod-
els and in dispersion-relation models based upon
QCD, yielding values in the 3-6-keV range for rl, .
Predictions for Zo and X2 have ranged from 4 to 8 keV
and from 1 to 2 keV, respectively.
The experimental observation of charmonium states in
photon-photon collisions is difficult because the two-
photon luminosity at these relatively high masses is low
and because the charmonium states have no dominant
decay modes. The decays are thought to proceed via two
intermediate gluons; consequently, branching fractions in
the most common hadronic final states are only a few
percent. Among photon-photon collision experiments,
severals have obtained only upper limits on I »(rl, ),
while Berger et al. , the PLUTO Collaboration, have re-
ported a signal in the K — Kgtr (Kg~ tr+tr ) channel.
A value has also been reported from the reaction pp

states have been reported from radiative ilr' decay" and
from pp annihilation. '
